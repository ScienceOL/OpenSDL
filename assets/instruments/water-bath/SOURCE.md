# WB 2 Water Bath original design

Geometry, dimensions, materials, motion pivots, display surfaces and operation fixtures are independently authored. No third-party CAD, meshes, textures, product identifiers or reference-model parameters are reused. These are generic educational instruments, not manufacturer replicas.

Rest-pose assembled width/depth/height: 300 / 283 / 228 mm. Moving envelope is checked separately from this resting size.

Original bath with 2 kg water charge at constant assumed 4180 J/(kg K), lumped 600 W heating, declared conductances and lid-dependent losses. No evaporation or calibrated uniformity claim.
