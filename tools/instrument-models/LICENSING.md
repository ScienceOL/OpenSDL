# Instrument authorship and licenses

New instrument geometry, each `build_<instrument>`/model-specific geometry module, model.yaml inputs, SOURCE.md and derived GLB/USD/thumbnail/model.graph declarations are **CC BY-NC 4.0**. Attribution: **OpenSDL / LiyanLabs**, with the asset's model ID, edition and source URL. A machine-readable SPDX license and the complete legal text travel with each package.

General exporter, geometric primitives, verification and runtime software retain the repository's MIT license. This separation is about the licensed work, not file extension. A script that specifies an instrument's actual design is part of that model's source, while a general cylinder/export helper is software tooling.

Analytical Balance AX240 **1.0.1 was already published under MIT**. Its prior grant remains available and is not retroactively restricted. Future CC editions may contain new work but do not withdraw rights to prior MIT material.

Provenance consists of declared authorship, exact input hashes, immutable source commits and OCI digests, and nonfunctional internal geometric constellations. Metadata may be stripped, geometry may be edited or rebuilt, and marks may be copied into unrelated work. Matching marks support an investigation; they do not independently establish infringement, authorship, legal liability or an unbroken chain of custody. Preserve original suspect bytes and compare full structure before drawing conclusions. No asset performs covert network reporting.
